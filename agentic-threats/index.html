Agentic threats explained

A nine-minute narrated walkthrough of the Google Threat Intelligence Group's AI threat tracker · November 2026

Interactive version (animation in the browser) Download MP4 (17 MB) Read the original report

What it covers

  1. The big picture. The mechanics of an attack haven't changed, stolen credentials and unpatched systems, but intrusions now run at machine speed.
  2. Multi-agent attack frameworks. CAIRN, PentAGI, ARTEX and others, what they can do, and why they currently trade stealth for speed.
  3. Automated vulnerability research. Patch-to-exploit pipelines, rising zero-day numbers and the PaperCut case.
  4. Influence operations. Turnkey platforms running more than 100,000 accounts, and why better content doesn't mean more impact.
  5. LLMjacking and LLM mixers. Stolen cloud and AI keys resold on underground storefronts.
  6. Government-backed groups, including a case that tampered with AI coding tool settings, and how Google is responding.

Source: Google Threat Intelligence Group and Mandiant, "GTIG AI Threat Tracker: Agentic Threat Activity Crosses a Significant Threshold", Google Cloud Blog, 17 November 2026. The video summarises that report and is not affiliated with Google.
