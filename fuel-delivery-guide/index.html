The fuel delivery model: a guide

A seven-minute narrated guide to a delivery scheduling model for 21 service stations, all supplied from a single home port · November 2026

Download MP4 (16 MB)

What it covers

  1. How it works inside. How the model plans deliveries and driver rosters for the stations.
  2. What it tells us. The baseline roster and what the results show.
  3. What moves the cost. The levers that change the cost of delivering fuel.
  4. Cost against dry tank days. Driving dry tank days down has a cost, and the relationship isn't a straight line, so the acceptable level is a pragmatic business call.
  5. Limits and open questions. What the model doesn't yet cover, and the questions to answer next.

The model is a proof of concept and is still being iterated, to get comfortable with its inputs and how it works. The cost curve in the video is illustrative, not a model result.
