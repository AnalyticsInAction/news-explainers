Working with AI explained

A ten-minute narrated explainer on Anthropic's report on how businesses are reorganising work around AI · November 2026

Interactive version (animation in the browser) Download MP4 (15 MB)

What it covers

  1. The study. 66 interviews at 58 US companies adopting AI, chosen for variety rather than to be representative, with self-reported value.
  2. Where the value comes from. High-value uses needed processes or roles to change; light-touch uses were mostly rated low value.
  3. Doing more, not spending less. 55% of use cases added output, 19% cut costs (mostly through reduced hiring), 19% gave no value yet.
  4. Jobs. AI-driven layoffs were rare, but people at two-thirds of companies reported hiring less, and nine companies cut contractors.
  5. Three approaches. Top-down automation (brittle, raises surveillance fears and expert resistance), bottom-up experimentation (domain experts thrive, but gains stay in pockets), and reorganising around AI.
  6. Bottlenecks. The most cited: too much AI output to check, staff resistance, and expert review. Customers and regulation were rarely cited.
  7. Reorganising. Broader roles, redeploying people to bottleneck tasks, and shared data and infrastructure.
  8. Six lessons. The video's own reading of the evidence for individuals, not the authors' recommendations.

Sources
